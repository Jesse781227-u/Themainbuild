Delete your data

Effective November 2026

You can ask Relay to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. This applies whether you are a business owner or a shopper who messaged a business that uses Relay.

How to ask

  • Business owners: send delete my data to the Relay Admin Bot on WhatsApp at +44 7529 762480. The bot will ask you to confirm.
  • Shoppers: send delete my data in your chat with the business, or email us (below) with the business name and the WhatsApp number or Instagram username you used.
  • By email: write to my.relay.africa@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". Send it from the account involved, or tell us how we can confirm the data is yours.

What happens next

  1. We confirm the request comes from you, usually by replying on the same WhatsApp number or Instagram account.
  2. Within 30 days we delete or anonymise your profile, saved addresses, conversation history and connected-account tokens.
  3. We keep only what the law requires, such as transaction records needed for tax and accounting. These are kept only for the required period and are not used for anything else.
  4. We send you a confirmation with a reference code you can quote if you have questions.

If you connected through Facebook or Instagram

If you remove Relay from your Facebook or Instagram settings, Meta notifies us and we start the same deletion process. You can use the confirmation code Meta shows you to check the status with us at my.relay.africa@gmail.com.

Business owners: deleting your account disconnects your WhatsApp and Instagram from Relay and stops the bots. Any orders already paid are still handled under the Terms of Service.
