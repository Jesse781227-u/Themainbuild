Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

Relay ("Relay", "we", "us") is operated by Relay, based at 9 Shadia Estate, Sholuyi, Gbagada, Lagos, Nigeria. Relay lets businesses sell to their customers inside WhatsApp and Instagram, using automated assistants ("bots"). This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

1. Who this policy covers, and our role

  • Business owners who use Relay through our Admin Bot on WhatsApp. For your data, Relay is the data controller.
  • Shoppers who message a business that uses Relay, on that business's WhatsApp number or Instagram account. For your data, the business is the controller and Relay processes your data on the business's behalf as its data processor. Questions about how a particular business uses your data should go to that business first, but you can always contact us too (section 11).

2. Data we collect

From business owners

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • Business details you give the Admin Bot: business name, category, address, delivery zones and fees, opening hours.
  • Products, prices, stock levels, product photos, expenses and debts you record.
  • The messages and voice notes you send to the Admin Bot.
  • Connection details for your WhatsApp Business number and Instagram account (account IDs and an access token, which we store encrypted).
  • Your payment-collection account details with our payment provider, Bachs.

From shoppers

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, or your Instagram account ID and username.
  • The messages, photos and voice notes you send to the business.
  • Order details: items, sizes, amounts, order history, and payment status and references.
  • Delivery details you give: name, alternative phone number, address, landmark and, if you share it, your location pin.
  • Your choices: whether the business may save your details for next time, and whether you agree to receive promotional messages.

We do not ask for, and you should not send, bank card numbers, PINs, BVN or passwords in chat.

3. How we use data

  • To run the bots: understand messages, show products, take orders, share payment details, confirm payments and arrange delivery or pickup.
  • To keep records for the business: orders, stock, sales, debts and daily summaries.
  • To send service messages: order confirmations, receipts, delivery updates and, for owners, alerts and daily reports.
  • To send promotional messages, only to shoppers who have opted in. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, keep the service secure, and meet our legal obligations.

Our legal bases under the NDPA are: performing a contract (running orders and the service), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement), consent (promotional messages and saving shopper details), and legal obligation (tax and accounting records).

4. Automated assistants and AI

Relay's bots use AI services to understand messages. Text messages are processed by Google's Gemini models to work out what you are asking for. Voice notes are transcribed by OpenAI before being handled the same way. We send these providers only what is needed to answer the message, and their output is used only to choose an action within Relay. The bots handle shopping and business tasks only. If a bot is unsure, it passes the conversation to a person at the business. AI can make mistakes: businesses review orders and payments, and you can always ask to speak to a person.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with:

  • The business you are dealing with (for shoppers).
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp and Instagram), which carries the messages.
  • Google (Firebase/Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage for data storage; Gemini for message understanding).
  • OpenAI (voice-note transcription).
  • Cloudflare (hosting and network security).
  • Bachs (payment collection and settlement).
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Each provider processes data under its own terms and security commitments.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers store or process data outside Nigeria. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the NDPA allows, such as contractual protections, and we transfer only what is needed.

7. How long we keep data

  • Technical message-delivery records: 30 days. These contain no message text.
  • Conversation state, such as an unfinished cart: until the order completes or the conversation goes quiet. We keep it for up to 30 days.
  • Orders, payments and accounting records: for as long as the business uses Relay, then 6 years to meet tax and legal obligations.
  • Shopper profiles saved with consent: until the shopper or the business asks us to delete them, or the business leaves Relay.

8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict how it is used, withdraw consent at any time, and receive your data in a portable format. See our Data Deletion page for how to ask. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access tokens for connected WhatsApp and Instagram accounts are encrypted at rest. Only Relay's servers can read or write the database, and access is limited to the business that owns the data.

10. Children

Relay is not meant for people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes and contact

We will post changes here and update the effective date. If a change is significant, business owners will hear about it on WhatsApp. For questions or requests, contact my.relay.africa@gmail.com or write to Relay, 9 Shadia Estate, Sholuyi, Gbagada, Lagos, Nigeria.
